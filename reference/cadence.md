# Cadence: Posting Rhythm & Time Windows

## Core Cadence Rules

### Rule 1: Max 3 Posts Per Day
The Author Diversity Scorer applies exponential decay:
- 1st post: 100% score
- 2nd post: 55% score
- 3rd post: 32.5% score
- 4th post: 21.3% score

**Implication**: Every post after the 3rd has severely diminished algorithmic competitiveness. Quality over quantity.

### Rule 2: Minimum 4-Hour Gap
Space posts at least 4 hours apart to:
- Avoid appearing in the same feed refresh cycle
- Give each post its own engagement window
- Reduce author diversity penalty impact

### Rule 3: Weekend ≠ Zero
Post 1-2 times on weekends. Algorithm doesn't stop, and competition is lower (fewer people posting = higher relative score).

## The 80-Hour Lifecycle

Every post follows this lifecycle:

```
Hour 0-2:   🔥 IGNITION — Maximum effort, respond to all replies
Hour 2-6:   📈 PEAK — Peak distribution, monitor engagement
Hour 6-24:  📊 PLATEAU — Steady distribution, reply to late comments
Hour 24-48: 📉 DECAY — Declining distribution, minimal effort
Hour 48-72: 🪦 TWILIGHT — Near-zero new impressions
Hour 72-80: ☠️ DEAD — AgeFilter removes from feed
```

### Actions per lifecycle phase:

**Hour 0-2 (Ignition):**
- Reply to every comment within minutes
- Share the post in relevant communities (Discord, Slack, Telegram)
- DM 3-5 contacts to check it out
- Quote tweet from secondary account (if applicable)

**Hour 2-6 (Peak):**
- Continue replying to comments
- Monitor engagement metrics
- Share to additional communities
- Engage with related posts to drive traffic

**Hour 6-24 (Plateau):**
- Reply to new comments (less urgency)
- Analyze early performance data
- Plan follow-up content if performing well

**Hour 24-80 (Decay):**
- Minimal active effort
- Post remains in feed organically
- Focus energy on next post

## Anti-Pattern: Over-Posting

**What happens when you post 5+ times a day:**

| Post # | Score Multiplier | Effective Reach |
|--------|-----------------|-----------------|
| 1 | 100% | Full potential |
| 2 | 55% | Half potential |
| 3 | 32.5% | 1/3 potential |
| 4 | 21.3% | 1/5 potential |
| 5 | 15.7% | 1/6 potential |

**Result**: Posts 4-5 are essentially wasted. The algorithm actively suppresses them.

**Better strategy**: Save those 2 extra posts for tomorrow when they'll get 100% score.

## Campaign Timing

### Giveaway Launch
- **Best day**: Thursday or Friday (people planning weekend projects)
- **Best time**: 9:00 AM (start of day)
- **Duration**: 48 hours (ends Saturday/Sunday evening)

### Product Launch Announcement
- **Best day**: Tuesday or Wednesday (mid-week, max attention)
- **Best time**: 9:00 AM
- **Follow-up**: 2:00 PM same day (supporting content)

### Benchmark/Comparison
- **Best day**: Monday or Tuesday (people planning their week)
- **Best time**: 1:00 PM (afternoon reading)
