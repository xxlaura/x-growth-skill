# Giveaway Design: Algorithm-Friendly Giveaways

Giveaways are growth accelerators that trigger multiple high-value signals simultaneously. Design them to maximize algorithm-friendly engagement.

## Algorithm Signal Targeting

A well-designed giveaway triggers:

| Signal | Weight | How to Trigger |
|--------|--------|---------------|
| `follow_author_score` | Very High | Require follow to enter |
| `repost_score` | Medium | Require repost to enter |
| `reply_score` | High | Require reply to enter |
| `share_via_dm_score` | Very High | Bonus entry for DM sharing |
| `dwell_score` | Medium | Longer post = more dwell |

## Giveaway Design Principles

### 1. Duration: 48 Hours
- Matches the algorithm's 80-hour window
- Creates urgency without being too short
- Gives enough time for viral spread

### 2. Entry Requirements: Layered
Layer requirements from easy to hard:
1. **Follow** (easiest) — triggers follow_author signal
2. **Repost** (easy) — triggers repost_score + distribution
3. **Reply** (medium) — triggers reply_score + engagement data
4. **DM share** (bonus) — triggers share_via_dm_score

### 3. Prize: Relevant & Valuable
- **Best**: Product credits (attracts real users)
- **Good**: Product credits + swag
- **OK**: Cash (attracts non-target audience)
- **Avoid**: Unrelated prizes (iPhones, etc.)

### 4. Question: Engagement Bait
The reply question should:
- Be fun to answer (not a survey)
- Generate user-generated content
- Reveal potential use cases
- Be specific enough to spark creativity

### 5. Winner Selection: Transparent
- Announce winner publicly
- Show selection criteria
- Tag winner in a follow-up post (extra engagement)
- Share winner's answer (creates a second post)

## Giveaway Amplification Tactics

### Pre-Launch (24h before)
- Tease the giveaway: "Something big coming tomorrow 👀"
- DM 5-10 KOLs asking them to share
- Post in relevant Discord/Telegram communities

### Launch
- Post at peak time (9-10 AM or 1-2 PM)
- Immediately reply to your own post with: "Full details in thread 👇" + additional info
- Share in 3-5 relevant communities
- DM key contacts to repost

### During (48h)
- Reply to every entry (boosts thread activity)
- Like and repost standout entries
- Post a reminder at 24h mark: "24 hours left to enter 👀"
- Share interesting entries as quote tweets

### Post-Giveaway
- Announce winner publicly
- Share the best entries as a thread: "Best answers from our giveaway 🧵"
- Thank everyone who participated
- Announce next giveaway date

## Giveaway Budget Guide

| Budget | Prize Structure | Expected Entries | Expected Followers |
|--------|----------------|-----------------|-------------------|
| $100 | 1 winner × $100 credits | 200-500 | 50-150 |
| $500 | 3 winners ($250/$150/$100) | 500-1,500 | 150-400 |
| $1,000 | 5 winners ($400/$300/$200/$100/$50) | 1,000-3,000 | 300-800 |
| $2,000 | 10 winners (graduated) | 2,000-5,000 | 500-1,500 |

**Cost per follower**: ~$1-3 (varies widely based on execution)

## Giveaway Compliance

### X Giveaway Rules:
- Must not require payment to enter
- Must state official rules
- Must comply with local laws
- Cannot require tagging excessive people

### Best Practices:
- Include "No purchase necessary" disclaimer
- State eligibility (age, region)
- Use a clear deadline with timezone
- Be transparent about winner selection
- Don't make entry requirements too onerous

## Giveaway Follow-Up Strategy

After the giveaway ends:

1. **Announce winner** (post + DM)
2. **Share best entries** as a thread (extra content)
3. **Offer consolation prize** to non-winners: "Didn't win? Here's $20 in free credits for everyone who entered → [link]"
4. **Announce next giveaway**: "Next giveaway drops [date]. Follow to not miss it"
5. **Nurture new followers**: Welcome DM with getting-started guide
