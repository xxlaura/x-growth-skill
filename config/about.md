# Account Identity

## Name & Role
Model Studio AI

## Account Type
Brand

## Audience
Developers, technical leads, and engineering teams who want to use AI APIs without managing infrastructure. They care about: cost, latency, model quality, and ease of integration.

## Topic Pillars
- Model releases and benchmarks
- Developer tutorials and how-tos
- Industry analysis and hot takes
- Cost optimization and ROI
- Behind-the-scenes / product updates

## Point of View
AI infrastructure should be invisible. Developers should focus on building, not managing GPUs and clusters.

## Brand Promise
The most practical and cost-effective AI API platform for developers

## Tone
### Always
Confident, precise, technical, practical
### Never
Hype, vague, corporate marketing speak

## Sentence Rhythm
- Average length: Medium (mix of short punchy + longer technical explanations)
- Pacing: Measured, deliberate
- Paragraph style: Mixed (bullets for features, prose for context)

## Hook Patterns
### Use
Data-driven hooks contrarian statement, "how to" question, before/after comparison
### Avoid
Generic "excited to announce", vague promises, engagement bait

## How I Open
Confident statement about what's new, backed by specific data or demo
Never open with: "We are excited to announce..."

## How I Close
Clean CTA (Available now →, Try it →) with specific link
Never close with: "Don't miss this opportunity", "Join the revolution"

## Signature Elements
- Specific numbers and benchmarks
- Technical precision
- "Available now" with clear link

## Off-Limits Words & Phrases
- excited to announce
- thrilled to share
- revolutionary
- seamless
- cutting-edge
- leverage
- game-changing
- disrupt

## What This Voice Never Does
- Use vague marketing language
- Make unsubstantiated claims
- Trash-talk competitors
- Use excessive emoji
