## Content Batch Production

**Recommended workflow:**

1. **Sunday evening**: Plan next week's content (topics, formats, times)
2. **Monday-Tuesday**: Produce all demo videos for the week (batch filming)
3. **Wednesday**: Write all text posts for Thu-Sun
4. **Daily**: 30 min for KOL replies (can't be batched)

**Tools:**
- Content calendar: Notion, Trello, or spreadsheet
- Scheduling: Typefully, Buffer, or TweetDeck
- Asset creation: Canva (images), Screen Studio (videos)

---

## Content Rotation Strategy

Don't repeat the same content type at the same time every day. Rotate:

| Time Slot | Mon | Tue | Wed | Thu | Fri |
|-----------|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 9 AM | Demo | Thread | Spotlight | Benchmark | Promo |
| 1 PM | Benchmark | Use Case | Demo | Use Case | Demo |
| 6 PM | Meme | Engagement | Meme | Giveaway | Meme |

This prevents audience fatigue and ensures variety.
